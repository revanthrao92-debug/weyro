MARKET FRICTION
The Automotive Repair Trilemma
02 / 10
1. Opaque Pricing
30-50% Unchecked Markup
  • Garages provide arbitrary, non-binding estimates
  • No visibility into genuine parts pricing vs labour
  • Vehicle owners trapped by local monopoly garages
2. Part Sourcing Bottlenecks
Weeks of Vehicle Downtime
  • Workshops restricted to local distributor catalogs
  • Frequent wrong-part deliveries and compatibility issues
  • Inability to access nationwide auto dismantling inventory
3. Zero Escrow Protection
High Financial Risk
  • Upfront deposits required with zero consumer recourse
  • No live courier or repair milestone accountability
  • Absence of formal warranty and dispute resolution

RESILIENCE ENGINEERING
Zero-Data-Loss Offline Architecture
08 / 10
1. Action Enqueueing
Every mutation (post broadcast, quote, booking, review) is buffered as an immutable QueuedMutation object with unique ID.
2. Native Storage Backup
Persisted to native device storage using @react-native-async-storage/async-storage, surviving app restarts and crashes.
3. FIFO Auto-Drain
Background worker drains queued actions in strict order with retry backoff and manual flush controls.
